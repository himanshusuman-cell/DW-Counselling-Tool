What to do
  • Warm, unhurried welcome — no pitch in the first five minutes
  • Ask the student directly, not the parent, why defence
  • Let the parent voice the fear they came with
  • Note the answer: passion, family background, or "no other option"
Opening words
"Defence mein jaane ka socha kab se hai — kisne inspire kiya?"
"Aaj hum kuch bechne nahi aaye. Sirf yeh samajhne aaye hain ki raasta kya hai."
"Aapke ghar mein kisi ne services join ki hai?"
💡 Parents rarely say it out loud, but the real question in their mind is safety and job security. Let them ask it. Answer with the structure of the exam and the officer career, not with emotion.

How to close
  • State the fee plainly — registration, security, books and infrastructure are already inside it
  • Break it into the installment plan before they ask

Common objections — and the honest answer
ObjectionWhat to say
"SSB toh clear hi nahi hoti"SSB carries 900 marks — exactly the same as the written paper. It is trainable. The 14-day offline SSB programme runs at Lucknow, Dehradun and Sikar, conducted by retired Brigadiers, Wing Commanders and Colonels with DIPR training.
"Medical mein reject ho gaya toh?"Get the medical checked early, before enrollment. Height, vision and past surgery are the usual issues. Knowing early protects the family from spending two years on a closed door.
"Fee zyada lag rahi hai"Show the installment split and the no-cost EMI. Then show the hours: 1600+ teaching hours in the 18-month programme, 600+ in the 6-month one.
"Agar select nahi hua toh degree?"NDA cadets receive a B.A., B.Sc. or B.Tech degree at Khadakwasla. CDS is a graduate entry, so the degree is already in hand.
